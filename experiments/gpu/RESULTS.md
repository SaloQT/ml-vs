- `gpu_kernel_ms` = device-only via CUDA events (post-warmup, median of 100).
- `gpu_e2e_ms`    = wallclock incl. one H->D copy of X and one D->H copy of Y.
- `cpu_ms`        = plain-JS Float32Array, median after warmup, adaptive iters.

Cells marked '—' = skipped (cpu would take >5 s) or N/A.
